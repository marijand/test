# Outreach Templates: Christian Prayer / Focus Mobile App
> Version 1.0 | Manual / review-before-send use only. Never batch-blast.

---

## Personalization Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Read the target site/podcast/newsletter before writing. Reference something specific and recent.
- Use the person's first name consistently.
- Lead with *their* work before mentioning yours.
- Keep each touch genuinely brief.
- Wait the full interval between follow-ups.
- Customize `{{personalized_opener}}` every single time — treat it as required, not optional.

**Don't:**
- Send the same subject line twice to the same contact.
- Mention competitors or make comparative claims.
- Over-explain the app in the first message — one sentence is enough.
- Follow up more than three times total on a cold thread.
- Add contacts to automation without their explicit opt-in.

## 1. Guest-Post Pitch (Christian / Faith Blogs)

### Subject-Line Variants
1. `A post idea for {{site_name}}: helping readers pray with fewer distractions`
2. `Guest piece for {{site_name}} — practical focus tips for daily devotion`
3. `Idea for your readers, {{first_name}}: a short guide to undistracted prayer`

### Personalized-Opener Guidance
Reference a specific recent article, series, or stated editorial gap. Example: "Your piece on *[article title]* really resonated — the section about [specific point] is something our users bring up constantly."

---

**Template**

Subject: `{{subject_line_variant}}`

Hi {{first_name}},

{{personalized_opener}}

I'm {{your_name}}, one of the people behind {{app_name}}, a prayer and focus app built for Christians who want to quiet the noise and stay present with God.

I'd love to contribute a practical, scripture-grounded piece for {{site_name}} — something like:
*"Five Ways to Reclaim Your Prayer Time in a Distracted World"*

No promotion, just genuinely useful content your readers can apply immediately. I'm happy to pitch two or three angles and let you pick.

Would a guest post be a fit right now?

Grateful for what you're building,
{{your_name}}

---

**Touch 2** (+5 days if no reply)

Subject: `Re: Guest piece idea for {{site_name}}`

Hi {{first_name}},

Just a brief follow-up on my note from last week. I know inboxes get full — no pressure at all.

If now isn't the right season editorially, I'd still love to stay connected. Is there a better time to revisit, or a preferred way to pitch ideas to you?

Either way, keep up the great work on {{site_name}}.

Grace,
{{your_name}}

---

**Touch 3** (+8 days after Touch 2 — final)

Subject: `Closing the loop — {{site_name}} guest post`

Hi {{first_name}},

I'll leave this thread here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If a guest contribution ever makes sense down the road, I'd be honored. I'll keep reading {{site_name}} in the meantime.

Blessings,
{{your_name}}

## 6. Unlinked-Mention / Broken-Link Reclamation

### Subject-Line Variants
1. `Quick note about {{app_name}} on {{site_name}}`
2. `Found a mention of {{app_name}} on your site — and a small fix to suggest`
3. `A helpful update for your {{page_title}} page, {{first_name}}`

### Personalized-Opener Guidance
Be specific: name the exact page, post title, or URL where the mention or broken link appears. Compliment the piece genuinely before raising the issue. For broken-link reclamation, briefly explain what the dead link pointed to and why {{app_name}} is a suitable replacement.

---

**Template — Unlinked Mention**

Subject: `{{subject_line_variant}}`

Hi {{first_name}},

{{personalized_opener}}

I noticed {{site_name}} mentioned {{app_name}} in your post "{{post_title}}" — thank you, that genuinely means a lot to our team.

One small thing: the mention doesn't include a link, which makes it a little harder for your readers to find us. If you ever update that post, a link to {{link}} would be wonderful — though absolutely no pressure.

Either way, thank you for the kind words. We'll keep working to earn them.

Gratefully,
{{your_name}}

---

**Template — Broken-Link Reclamation**

Subject: `{{subject_line_variant}}`

Hi {{first_name}},

{{personalized_opener}}

I came across your page "{{page_title}}" ({{page_url}}) while researching resources for Christian prayer. I noticed the link to [dead resource name] ({{broken_url}}) appears to be returning a 404.

{{app_name}} covers similar ground — it's a free Christian prayer and focus app — and might serve as a useful replacement for your readers: {{link}}

Happy to share more about it if helpful. Either way, thought a heads-up on the broken link might be useful.

Blessings,
{{your_name}}

---

**Touch 2 — for both variants** (+5 days if no reply)

Subject: `Re: {{app_name}} mention / link on {{site_name}}`

Hi {{first_name}},

Just a brief follow-up on my note from earlier. No urgency — I know site updates take time. If there's any additional info I can provide (a short description, a logo, an app preview image), I'm happy to send it over.

Thank you for keeping {{site_name}} such a valuable resource,
{{your_name}}

---

**Touch 3** (+7 days after Touch 2 — final)

Subject: `Leaving this here — {{site_name}} link note`

Hi {{first_name}},

Last note from me on this. The link/info is here whenever it's useful: {{link}}. Thank you for everything you publish — it serves a lot of people.

Warmly,
{{your_name}}
